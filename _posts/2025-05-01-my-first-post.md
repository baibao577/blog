# 👋 Hi, I’m Knot

After years of reading other people’s blogs, I’ve finally decided to start writing mine. This space is part technical journal, part personal reflection — a place where I can share not just what I know, but also who I am.

## About Me (The Human Side)

I’m Thai, born and raised in Bangkok. I grew up surrounded by computers, internet cafés, and open-source forums. That curiosity turned into a career in software — and eventually leadership — but deep down, I’m still the same kid who loves tinkering and building things from scratch.

Beyond work, I’m a bit of a mix:
- 🥋 I practice Muay Thai — it keeps me grounded and focused.
- 📚 I’m obsessed with storytelling, especially sci-fi and fantasy worlds.
- 🛠️ I build small side projects not because I have to, but because I *want* to.

## Why I’m Writing

For a long time, I felt like blogging had to be polished, "expert-level" content. But I’ve come to realize it’s more about *capturing the journey* — not just the outcomes.

This blog will be where I:
- Share lessons learned (technical and non-technical)
- Reflect on my path — the wins, failures, and weird turns
- Explore ideas around engineering, product, leadership, and creativity

## What to Expect

I won’t promise a fixed schedule or a single theme. Some days it might be about a code refactor I’m proud of. Others, it might be about burnout, work-life balance, or a Muay Thai sparring session that taught me something about resilience.

If you’re into honest, thoughtful writing with a bit of a builder’s soul — welcome.

Thanks for being here.

— Knot
